# Sugar Cubes Hopes website

## Files
- `index.html` — all six website sections and content
- `style.css` — responsive styling and brand colours
- `script.js` — mobile navigation, dessert filters, details dialog, and demo enquiry form
- `assets/sugar-cubes-hopes-logo.png` — your original logo, copied without redesign

## Run
Open `index.html` in a browser. Internet access is needed for Google Fonts and the remote dessert photos.

## Brand colours
- Chocolate brown: `#582D0D`
- Golden yellow: `#F6C15C`
- Cream backgrounds

## Notes
- This is a dessert showcase website: it has no cart, checkout, prices, or online ordering.
- The enquiry form is front-end only. Connect it to an email service or backend to receive messages.
- Replace the sample remote dessert photos with your own shop photos before publishing.
