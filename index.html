About Sugar Cubes

Our Sweet Story

Sugar Cubes began its journey with a passion for creating delicious and memorable dessert experiences. Our main branch is located in R.S. Puram, Coimbatore.

Expanding our sweet journey, Sugar Cubes Hopes opened its doors on 23rd October 2026. Born from a passion for baking and a love for sweet creations, Sugar Cubes Hopes crafts fresh, high-quality desserts using premium and natural ingredients to make everyday moments more special.
