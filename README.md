# 🍰 Sugar Cubes Hopes — Dessert Showcase Website

Official showcase website for **Sugar Cubes Hopes**, crafted with love and premium natural ingredients in Coimbatore.

---

## ✨ Features
- **3D Hero Showcase**: Full-bleed 3D illuminated signboard background with smooth Ken Burns zoom animation.
- **★ Signature (Must Try) Filter**: Curated signature desserts highlighted by default, with category filters for *All Items, Shakes, Tres Leches, Cakes, Brownies, Cookies,* and *Platters*.
- **Authentic Menu Catalog**: 23 authentic dishes and specialty shakes extracted from the official menu, optimized with WebP for lightning-fast loading.
- **Interactive Google Maps Integration**: Direct tap-to-navigate links with Google Maps pins in About, Contact, and Footer pointing to the verified Hope College location.
- **Custom Cake Enquiries**: Form and quick links for handcrafted birthday, anniversary, and celebration cakes.
- **Luxury Multi-Column Footer**: Complete brand story, quick navigation, menu shortcuts, business hours, and social channels.
- **Fully Responsive**: Seamlessly optimized for mobile, tablet, laptop, and ultra-wide screens.
